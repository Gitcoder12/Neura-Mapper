# Neura-Mapper — Company and Team Fit

Matches represent potential technical fit, not claims of use, endorsement, clinical adoption or partnership.

## Project thesis

A simple 3D grid pathfinder that finds a route through cells labelled as open, risky or blocked.

## NVIDIA

**Relevant public technical areas:** 3D simulation, robotics and accelerated computing.

**Potential roles:** Research Engineer, Robotics Software Engineer, Simulation Engineer, 3D Systems Engineer or ML Engineer.

**Evidence to build:** Current implementation is a small in-memory 3D grid pathfinder with manually assigned safe/warning/critical voxels. It has no medical-image ingestion, anatomical registration, clinical validation, uncertainty model or clinical workflow integration. The repository must be described as an educational/research prototype, not a neurosurgical navigation system.
